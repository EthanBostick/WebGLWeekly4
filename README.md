### Reflection questions

1. Which scene was the easiest to create?:
   - the easiest one was the moon scene, right after finding all the knobs and controls for the sunset scene it was easy to just change those values around
2. Which scene looked the most realistic?
   - the campfire scene; imagining the campfire just next to the left side of the pyramid works pretty well for it seeming realistic. It also does not suffer from the lack of shadow casting we have in the other scenes.
3. How did ambient light affect the mood of each scene?
   - higher ambient light made each scene feel more sterile similar to having flood lights or being in a doctors office. The lower values make a cozier game feel.
4. How did light direction affect visibility?
   - the light direction mostly just affected the object shadows and which face was illuminated. It seemed like the ambient light was more responsible for the overall scene brightness
5. Which color produced the strongest visual effect?
   - the orange fire light, it had the strongest impression on me
6. Why do game developers spend so much time adjusting lighting?
   - lighting effects the mood of the players and also gives vibes to where ever they are used. It is important to give the right feeling to the player depending on the scene
7. If you were making a horror game, what lighting would you use and why?
   - depending on the scene, I would use a lot of dim ambient light but I would also, when needed, use bright cold white lights. The dim ambient lights give the impression of lurking shadows wherever there is no light, as the ambient light does not cast as far. The cold bright lights give that spooky ah ah feeling of a given environment being unfriendly, cold, and having hospital vibes.
